# 🌰 nut-oclock

Nut O'clock is a silly little joke project that tracks every time zone on Earth and counts down to midnight on November 1st, 2026, wherever that happens to be next. When the clock strikes, there's confetti, a message, and the countdown moves on to the next time zone, until the whole world has made it through.

It's a small celebration of the end of a certain... *ahem*... event that takes place every October.

## How it works

- Starts with the first place on Earth to reach midnight on November 1st (Kiribati) and works its way around the globe.
- Shows example countries for each time zone, rotating through them.
- Time zones and daylight saving changes are handled by the browser, so no offsets are hardcoded.
- Single HTML file, no dependencies, no tracking. Just open `index.html`.
